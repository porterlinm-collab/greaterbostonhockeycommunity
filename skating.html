Skate Sharpening

Warrior Arena offers skate sharpening available 7 days a week during public hours, found in Skate Rental, Level 2.

  • $12/pair
  • 10-pass: $110

Public Skating

Warrior Arena hosts Friday Night Rock 'n Skate, 9:00 – 9:50 PM, with a DJ and lighting. Admission is $18 (all ages), skate rental $6. Pre-registration is preferred and guarantees a spot; schedules are subject to change, so check the current calendar before you go.

FMC Ice Sports also offers public skating sessions across their Greater Boston area rinks.

Public Hockey & Stick and Puck

Warrior Arena Public Hockey admission (all ages): $15 weekday, $18 weekend, holiday rate $18 (includes April vacation week). Goalies skate free. Full equipment is required.

  • 10-admission pass: $150 (does not include skate rental; valid 1 year from purchase)
  • Season passes: $300 individual, $400 two-person, $500 family (up to 4); includes skate rentals and covers both Public Skating and Public Hockey; valid 1 year from purchase

For stick and puck, check out the Summer Women's Stick & Puck at Viglirolo Rink in Belmont, Wednesdays and Fridays, 9:00 – 11:00 AM (also listed on our Programs & Calendar page).
